I am a postdoctoral researcher fellow at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca){:target="_blank"} and [Mila - Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en){:target="_blank"}, advised by Prof. [David Rolnick](https://davidrolnick.com){:target="_blank"}.
My current research focuses on developing machine learning methods that can help tackle the climate crisis. I develop causal representation learning and identifiable methods for dynamical systems, to improve the robustness and interpretability of data-driven climate models, and facilitate scientific discovery from observations of physical systems. I also develop denoising and active learning methods for bioacoustics recordings, to facilitate the processing of large recordings. I am mostly interested in modeling real-world physical data using tools and techniques from signal processing, optimization, probabilistic modeling, machine learning, and dynamical systems. 
I am a recipient of the [Fonds de Recherche du Québec](https://frq.gouv.qc.ca){:target="_blank"} Postdoctoral Scholarship. Previously, my research was funded by the [IVADO Exploratory Projects Program](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/liam/){:target="_blank"}

I completed my Ph.D. in September 2024 at Columbia University, under the supervision of [Liam Paninski](https://ivado.ca/en/scholarships-and-grants/ivado-exploratory-projects-program/){:target="_blank"}. My Ph.D. work lied at the intersection of statistics and computational neuroscience. I focused on analyzing large-scale neural recordings and developing data-driven approaches to analyze and decode electrophysiological recordings. 
Before my PhD, I graduated from the Ecole Polytechnique in 2017 before getting a Master’s degree in Statistics at Stanford University, in 2019. During my Master, I was a research assistant under the supervision of [Mykel Kochenderfer](https://mykel.kochenderfer.com){:target="_blank"} where I built a RNN-based model for predicting Sleep Quality from EEG recordings, and did an internship at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit at UCL under the supervision of [Maneesh Sahani](https://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~maneesh/){:target="_blank"} where I developed an approach to learn an interpretable model of latent continuous dynamical systems.

I care about reproducible, rigorous and transparent research, and also believe that science should be inclusive and accessible. I'm concerned about the potential harms of AI, and co-organize the [Critical Science Reading Group](https://criticalscience.github.io){:target="_blank"} to better understand the connections between science and society at large.
